UK Visa & Sponsorship Routes

Dania Maids UK assists with legal guidance for domestic staffing in Great Britain.

01

Overseas Domestic Worker Visa

For maids, housekeepers, cooks, and nannies who have been employed by a household overseas for at least 1 year and are traveling with the employer to the UK.

  • Valid for up to 6 months
  • Allows changing domestic employers within the UK
  • Protected by full UK employment rights
02

Care Worker & Senior Carer Sponsorship

For individuals trained in elderly care, home support, or healthcare assistance under UK Licensed Sponsors.

  • 3 to 5 Year Sponsored Visa
  • Pathway to UK Permanent Residency (ILR)
  • Full sponsorship opportunities
